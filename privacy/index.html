01 LEGAL

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CleanTool processes information when you use our mobile app, website, and connected services.

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Who we are

CleanTool is a device-cleanup and inbox-management service. In this policy, “CleanTool,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the CleanTool service. If you have a privacy question or request, contact us at support@curatedx.io.

2. Information we process

Account and app information

When you choose to sign in, we process your sign-in provider identifier, name, email address, and the information needed to keep your CleanTool account secure and associated with your device. We also process app preferences, support messages you send us, and technical information needed to keep the app working.

Photos, contacts, and private files

CleanTool accesses your photo library and contacts only after you grant device permission. Photo and contact review happens on your device. Contact backups, app preferences, and Secret Library items are stored locally on your device; Secret Library items are encrypted on that device. We do not upload your photo library, contact list, or Secret Library contents to our backend just to provide those on-device features.

Gmail information

Gmail connection is optional. If you choose to connect a Gmail account, we process the Gmail address, encrypted authorization credentials, and a bounded mailbox projection needed to provide the features you request. This includes limited message metadata such as sender and recipient information, subject, snippet, labels, categories, dates, sizes, attachment count, and service identifiers. CleanTool does not retain Gmail message bodies or HTML, raw link targets, or attachment contents. Full message content is requested only in transient memory after you explicitly open an eligible message-detail feature. CleanTool uses this information to show cleanup options, apply the Gmail action you explicitly choose, and run an Auto Cleanup schedule only when you turn one on.

Purchases, referrals, and payouts

Subscription providers process payment information. CleanTool does not receive or store your full payment card number. If you participate in our referral program, PromoteKit may process your affiliate profile, referral activity, commission status, and payout email. Please also review the applicable provider terms and notices before enrolling. On our website, a referral link may allow a referral-attribution provider to store or read a referral identifier so a qualifying web checkout can be attributed to the correct affiliate.

Device, permission, and diagnostics information

Depending on what you enable, we process your device permissions and identifiers needed for app notifications, widgets, security, and support. CleanTool may request access to photos and videos, contacts, notifications, the camera for QR features, and Face ID or device biometrics for Secret Library. Biometric information is handled by your device and is not sent to our backend.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide, secure, maintain, and improve CleanTool;
  • authenticate you and keep your account and connected mailbox separate from other users;
  • show the photo, contact, Gmail, subscription, referral, and notification features you choose to use;
  • complete and record the cleanup action you explicitly request;
  • process subscriptions, referrals, support requests, fraud prevention, and legal obligations; and
  • communicate important service, security, or account messages.

4. Gmail data and your controls

CleanTool does not permanently delete Gmail messages. When you choose a supported cleanup action, a message may be moved to Gmail Trash, archived, marked read or unread, or reported as spam according to the action you selected. You can disconnect a mailbox in CleanTool. Disconnecting revokes CleanTool’s usable Gmail access for that mailbox and stops future Auto Cleanup activity.

We do not use Gmail data to serve advertising, sell it, or allow humans to read it for advertising purposes. We do not transfer Gmail data to third parties except as needed to provide or improve these user-facing features, comply with law, or protect against abuse and security incidents.

5. Service providers and sharing

We use carefully selected service providers to operate CleanTool. Depending on the feature you use, these may include Google for account and Gmail authorization, Supabase for authenticated backend services and encrypted credential storage, Apple and Google for platform services, Expo when you enable push notifications, Stripe and Superwall for web and subscription services, and PromoteKit for referral-program administration. Providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to us and you.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights, safety, and security of CleanTool and others, or in connection with a lawful business transfer.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account and connected-service information only for as long as needed to provide CleanTool, meet our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Gmail credentials are retained while a mailbox remains connected and, when the disconnect or account-deletion process completes successfully, are revoked and removed. The mailbox projection is limited to the data needed for CleanTool’s current review and cleanup features and is designed for bounded retention, subject to operational and legal needs. We retain limited operation and security records for bounded periods to prevent duplicate actions, investigate failures, and maintain service safety.

You can delete your CleanTool account in the app. When that process completes successfully, account deletion revokes connected Gmail access and removes active CleanTool account data. It does not delete content from your Gmail account, photo library, or device contacts. To remove locally stored CleanTool data, use the app’s clear-device option or delete the app. Referral or payout providers may retain records when necessary for commissions, fraud prevention, tax, or legal compliance.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. For example, backend-held Gmail authorization credentials are encrypted and are not sent to the mobile app. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can control device permissions in your device settings, disconnect Gmail, opt out of nonessential notifications through device settings, and delete your CleanTool account in the app. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we control, email support@curatedx.io.

9. Children

CleanTool is not directed to children. Do not use CleanTool if you are not old enough to consent to data processing under the laws that apply to you. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as CleanTool changes. We will post the updated version here and revise the “Last updated” date. For questions, requests, or concerns, contact support@curatedx.io.

Google API Services disclosure. CleanTool’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
